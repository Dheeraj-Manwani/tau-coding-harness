Packs are one-off purchases. No subscription, no commitment, and the credits do
not expire.

## The packs

| Credits | Price | Per credit |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | ₹49 | ₹0.49 |
| 500 | ₹199 | ₹0.40 |
| 2,000 | ₹699 | ₹0.35 |

Prices are in INR, inclusive of applicable taxes. Payment is processed by
Razorpay.

## Buying one

From your billing page: pick a pack, pay, and the credits land on your account.
You do not need a subscription and you do not need to be on PRO.

Purchased credits go into your **bonus** bucket, which never expires.

## Packs versus PRO

| | Credit packs | PRO |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per credit | ₹0.35–0.49 | ₹0.20 |
| Expiry | Never | End of the billing cycle |
| Commitment | None | Monthly |
| Project cap | Still 3 on Free | Unlimited |

PRO is cheaper per credit and packs are more flexible. The deciding question is
whether your usage is steady.

**Build in bursts?** Packs. You pay more per credit but you never lose an unused
allotment, and a quiet month costs nothing.

**Build most weeks?** PRO. 5,000 credits for ₹1,499 still costs less per credit than a pack,
and it lifts the project cap.

**Both?** That works: plan credits are spent before pack credits, so your
expiring allotment is always the one being drawn down. A pack is a reserve that
survives the cycle.

→ [Credits](/docs/billing/credits) for the full spend order.

## When a pack is the right move mid-build

A build that stops because you ran out of credits has not lost anything. Buy a
pack, then send a follow-up: the project picks up from where it stopped, same
files, same sandbox if it is still alive.

> [!TIP]
> If you are starting something large on Max, check your balance first. A build
> refuses to start below a minimum available balance, and topping up before you
> begin is less annoying than topping up halfway through.

## Refunds

Credits are consumed as work is done. An unused pack purchase is a payment
question, not a credit question: see [Refunds](/docs/billing/refunds).
