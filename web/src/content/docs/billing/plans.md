Two plans. Both have every feature; they differ in credits and project count.

| | **Free** | **PRO** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | ₹0 | ₹1,499 / month |
| Credits | 25, once, at signup | 5,000 per billing cycle |
| Projects at a time | 3 | Unlimited |
| Effort tiers | All three, including Max | All three |
| AI gateway | Yes | Yes |
| GitHub push | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Standard | Priority |

## What Free actually gets you

Everything. There is no feature behind the paywall — no locked effort tier, no
disabled gateway, no watermark.

What you get less of is credits and project slots. 200 credits is enough for
several small builds, and 3 projects is enough to keep one real thing and two
experiments.

> [!NOTE]
> The 200 credits are a **one-time grant**, not a monthly allowance. There is no
> daily or monthly refill on Free. When they are gone, buy a pack, redeem a code,
> or upgrade.

## What PRO changes

**5,000 credits on your billing date, every cycle.** These are *plan* credits and
they expire at the end of the cycle — they do not roll over. That is what makes
them cheap per credit.

**Unlimited projects.** No 3-project cap.

**Priority support.**

Everything else is identical, because everything else was already available.

## Whether to upgrade

Upgrade when you are regularly out of credits or regularly deleting a project to
make room. Those are the only two things the plan changes.

If you build in bursts — a lot one week, nothing the next — [credit
packs](/docs/billing/credit-packs) are the better fit. Pack credits never expire;
plan credits do.

## Plan credits expire

Unused plan credits are gone at the end of the cycle. This is worth knowing if you
subscribe and then have a quiet month.

Because credits are spent [free → plan → bonus](/docs/billing/credits), your plan
allotment is always drawn down before any pack you bought, so the expiring
credits are the ones being used.

## Payment

PRO is a Razorpay subscription, billed monthly in INR inclusive of applicable
taxes. Cancel any time from your billing page.

Cancelling stops future billing. Credits already granted for the current cycle
stay until the cycle ends; they expire then, as always.

## Project cap on downgrade

If you hold more than 3 projects and stop being PRO, the cap applies to
*creating* new ones — existing projects are not deleted. You will not be able to
create another until you are back under 3.

> [!TIP]
> Push anything you care about to GitHub before deleting a project to free a slot.
> Deletion is not reversible. See [Exporting](/docs/ship/exporting).
