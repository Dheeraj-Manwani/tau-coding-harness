---
title: Real images, not grey boxes
date: 2026-07-25
---

The agent can now find and place actual images while it builds.

- **Image search** — tau looks for a photograph that fits instead of leaving a
  placeholder.
- **Asset download** — it fetches the image into your project.
- **Dimension reading** — it checks the real size before laying it out, so the
  aspect ratio is right the first time rather than after a follow-up.

The practical difference: a landing page tau builds you arrives looking like a
page, not like a wireframe.
