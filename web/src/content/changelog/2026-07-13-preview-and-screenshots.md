---
title: Start preview, and project thumbnails
date: 2026-07-13
---

- **Start preview.** Sandboxes do not live forever, and a dead one used to mean
  re-prompting to get your app running again — spending credits on an agent you did
  not need. The button now rebuilds a running environment directly: reconnect or
  provision a sandbox, rehydrate it from your stored files, start the dev server,
  hand back a URL. No model thinks about it.
- **Project screenshots.** When a build finishes, tau captures the running preview
  and uses it as the project card's thumbnail — so a list of six projects is
  readable at a glance instead of six identical rectangles.
